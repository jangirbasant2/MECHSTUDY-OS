## SUBJECT 1: Advanced Engineering Mathematics-I

**Subject Code:** 3ME2-01  
**Credits:** 3  
**Maximum Marks:** 150  
**Internal Assessment:** 30  
**End-Term Examination:** 120  
**Examination Duration:** 3 hours

### Unit 1: Numerical Methods – 1
- Finite differences.
- Relations between operators.
- Interpolation using Newton's forward and backward difference formulae.
- Gauss's forward and backward interpolation formulae.
- Stirling's formula.
- Interpolation with unequal intervals.
- Newton's divided difference formula.
- Lagrange's interpolation formula.
- Numerical differentiation.
- Numerical integration:
  - Trapezoidal rule.
  - Simpson's 1/3 rule.
  - Simpson's 3/8 rule.

### Unit 2: Numerical Methods – 2
- Numerical solution of ordinary differential equations:
  - Taylor's series method.
  - Euler's method.
  - Modified Euler's method.
  - Fourth-order Runge–Kutta method for first- and second-order equations.
  - Milne's predictor-corrector method.
  - Adams predictor-corrector methods.
- Solution of polynomial and transcendental equations:
  - Bisection method.
  - Newton–Raphson method.
  - Regula-Falsi method.

### Unit 3: Laplace Transform
- Definition and existence of Laplace transforms.
- Properties and formulae.
- Unit step function.
- Dirac delta function.
- Heaviside function.
- Laplace transforms of periodic functions.
- Inverse Laplace transforms using different methods.
- Convolution theorem.
- Evaluation of integrals using Laplace transforms.
- Solving ordinary differential equations using Laplace transforms.

### Unit 4: Fourier Transform
- Fourier complex transform.
- Fourier sine transform.
- Fourier cosine transform.
- Properties and formulae.
- Inverse Fourier transforms.
- Convolution theorem.
- Applications to partial differential equations:
  - One-dimensional heat equation.
  - One-dimensional wave equation.

### Unit 5: Z-Transform
- Definition.
- Properties and formulae.
- Convolution theorem.
- Inverse Z-transform.
- Applications to difference equations.

## SUBJECT 8: Materials Testing Lab

**Subject Code:** 3ME4-22  
**Credits:** 1.5  
**Maximum Marks:** 75  
**Internal Assessment:** 45  
**End-Term Examination:** 30  
**Practical course:** 0L + 0T + 3P

### Experiments and Practical Topics

1. Study crystal structures using models:
   - BCC.
   - FCC.
   - HCP.
   - Tetrahedral voids.
   - Octahedral voids.

2. Material identification of approximately 50 common items.

3. Specimen preparation for metallographic or microstructural examination:
   - Cutting.
   - Grinding.
   - Polishing.
   - Etching.

4. Comparative study of microstructures of specimens such as mild steel, grey cast iron, brass, and copper.

5. Heat-treatment experiments:
   - Annealing.
   - Normalizing.
   - Quenching.
   - Case hardening.
   - Comparison of hardness before and after treatment.

6. Study microstructure and hardness of steel at different cooling rates. Examine white cast iron microstructure.

7. Tensile, compressive, shear, and torsion tests to determine mechanical properties under the relevant loading conditions.

8. Determine Rockwell, Vickers, and Brinell hardness.

9. Perform impact testing and determine resilience.

10. Perform fatigue testing and determine fatigue strength.

11. Perform bending tests and determine Young's modulus of elasticity through beam deflection.

12. Perform creep testing using a creep-testing machine.
